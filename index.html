About

I work as a Failure Analysis (FA) Technician at Advanced Energy in Laguna. My day is bench troubleshooting: tracing a failed unit down to the part, the process step or the test that caused it, then reporting it clearly to engineers and site leads.

My direction is power electronics together with electrical work: supplies, bridges, motor control, solar systems and the wiring and drawings that go with them. I am studying it by building small tools and diagrams I can check by hand.

I trained in Electromechanics Technology at Dualtech and I keep learning. Next, I plan to pursue a degree through CHED ETEEAP at Manuel S. Enverga University Foundation. I started coding to remove repeated paperwork, so every tool here is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript that runs offline on a phone.

Breadboard
